How to Apply for IB School Admission

The admission process for IB school at The NEST School is designed to be smooth, transparent, and parent-friendly. We offer IB admissions for Early Years, Primary Years Programme, and IB Diploma Programme.

01

Submit Enquiry Form

Begin your IB school admission journey by filling out the online enquiry form. Our admissions team will guide you through the next steps.

02

School Interaction / Assessment

Students may be invited for an age-appropriate interaction or academic readiness assessment, depending on the grade level.

03

Document Submission

Parents submit required documents including academic records, identification, and transfer certificates (if applicable).

04

Admission Confirmation

Once eligibility is confirmed, admission is secured upon fee payment as per our IB school fee structure.

05

Welcome to The NEST School

Students begin their international education journey under the globally recognised IB curriculum.
