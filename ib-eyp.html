IB Early Years Programme (Nursery)

Learning in the IB Early Years Programme is guided by experienced educators who create meaningful experiences through exploration, storytelling, creative play, music, movement, and sensory activities. The curriculum encourages independence, confidence, and international-mindedness while respecting each child’s individual pace of development. Parents are supported through regular communication, ensuring a strong partnership between home and school.

Driven by an amalgamation of leading educational curricula, The NEST School offers a distinctive Nursery Programme, personalised to the learning needs of every learner. Concepts fundamental to everyday life are unfolded through play and inquiry by incorporating the best of Reggio Emilia, Montessori and Waldorf methods while keeping the IB PYP curriculum as the basis of instructional delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions – IB Early Years Programme (EYP)

The IB Early Years Programme (EYP) is part of the International Baccalaureate framework designed for young learners in their early childhood years. It focuses on play-based, inquiry-driven learning that supports cognitive, social, emotional, and physical development.

The IB Early Years Programme at The NEST School is designed for nursery and early childhood learners, typically between the ages of 2.5 and 5 years.

Yes, the IB Early Years Programme follows the International Baccalaureate philosophy, which is globally recognised for its high standards in education and holistic child development.

The programme builds strong foundational skills such as communication, collaboration, problem-solving, and independence, preparing children for a smooth transition into the IB Primary Years Programme (PYP).

Learning is inquiry-based and play-centred, guided by trained educators who create meaningful learning experiences through exploration, storytelling, creative activities, and hands-on engagement.

Parents are active partners in the learning journey through regular communication, updates on student progress, and opportunities to engage with the school community.

Yes, The NEST School in Chennai offers the IB Early Years Programme as part of its international curriculum pathway from Early Years to Grade 12.
