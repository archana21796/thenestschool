IB Diploma Programme (DP) – Candidacy (Grades 11–12)

While Cambridge AS & A Levels offer exceptional flexibility and subject depth, the IB Diploma Programme (IBDP), emphasizes on the breadth of knowledge, critical thinking, and global-mindedness

By offering both pathways, The NEST School ensures that every student has the opportunity to choose the qualification that best matches their interests, learning style, and future goals. Regardless of the curriculum, The NEST School will provide the guidance, support, and world-class education needed to open doors to top universities and careers worldwide.

At The NEST School, we believe in empowering every learner to pursue a pathway that nurtures their goals and aspirations. As an interested school for the IB Diploma Programme (IBDP), we are committed to expanding future-ready opportunities for our learners. After completing Cambridge IGCSE, students may continue their academic journey through either the Cambridge International AS & A Levels or the IB Diploma Programme —both globally respected qualifications recognized by leading universities worldwide.

Reimagining Learning

Real World Applications

The AS/A level & IBDP Programs at The NEST School not only provide rigorous academic knowledge but also nurture critical thinking, research skills, and a global outlook essential for success beyond the classroom. In line with the school’s vision, these programs empower learners to approach challenges with resilience, adaptability, and confidence—qualities that prepare them to thrive in a dynamic and ever-evolving world.

Identifying Talents

The AS/A level & IBDP Programs at The NEST School provide diverse opportunities for learners to engage in self-reflection, explore varied academic and co-curricular experiences, and seek constructive feedback from peers and mentors. Our experienced facilitators carefully observe and guide students to recognize patterns of strength and interest, helping uncover their innate talents. These insights are then channeled towards purposeful development—fostering both personal growth and future readiness in an ever-changing global context

Community Outreach

Emphasizing the importance of becoming compassionate, informed global citizens, the AS/A Level & IBDP Programs at The NEST School foster meaningful engagement through the CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service) framework. Learners participate in purposeful community initiatives that encourage empathy, leadership, and collaboration. Through volunteerism and hands-on experiences, they partner with local communities to identify needs, design sustainable solutions, and create lasting social impact—strengthening their sense of responsibility and connection to the world around them.

Frequently Asked Questions – IB Diploma Programme (DP) Candidacy

The IB Diploma Programme (DP) is a rigorous, internationally recognised curriculum designed for students aged 16 to 19. It focuses on academic excellence, critical thinking, global awareness, and holistic development.

IB DP Candidacy means that The NEST School is in the process of becoming an authorised IB World School for the Diploma Programme and is implementing the IB framework in accordance with IB standards and practices.

The IB Diploma Programme at The NEST School is offered for students in Grades 11 and 12, following the Cambridge IGCSE curriculum in Grades 9 and 10.

Yes, the IB Diploma Programme is globally recognised by leading universities and colleges for its academic rigour, research focus, and emphasis on independent learning.

The programme develops advanced research skills, time management, critical thinking, and interdisciplinary understanding, preparing students for success in university and beyond.

Teaching follows IB-aligned practices such as inquiry-based learning, concept-driven instruction, formative assessments, and student reflection, guided by trained educators.
