Cambridge IGCSE Programme (Grades 9–10)

The Cambridge IGCSE Programme (Grades 9–10) at The NEST School, Chennai offers a globally recognised curriculum that builds strong academic foundations, critical thinking, and independent learning skills. Aligned with the Cambridge Assessment International Education framework, the programme emphasises conceptual understanding, application-based learning, and subject depth. Students are guided by experienced educators in a supportive international school environment, preparing them confidently for IB Diploma Programme (Grades 11–12) and future global pathways.

Wherever your child dreams of going and whichever career they wish to pursue, The NEST School is dedicated to helping them get there with the globally respected Cambridge IGCSE. It is a truly international curriculum that provides unparalleled flexibility, offering a wide range of subjects and learning methods. This allows students to tailor their education to their interests and strengths.

With Cambridge IGCSE, students are assessed in multiple ways, including written exams, oral tests, coursework, and practical evaluations. This diverse approach ensures that every student has the opportunity to succeed in their own unique way.

Reimagining Learning

Real World Applications

Cambridge IGCSE not only provides academic knowledge but also equips students with the skills and mindset required to succeed in the real world. Part of The NEST School’s vision is to encourage learners to approach challenges with resilience and adaptability, helping them to cope with changing circumstances, which is a crucial skill in today’s fast-paced and evolving job market.

Identifying Talents

The NEST School offers a range of activities that involves self-reflection, exploring the different activities, and seeking feedback within each other to recognize areas where the learners naturally excel. Our trained facilitators observe patterns of success help reveal innate abilities, which can be developed for personal and professional growth.

Community Outreach

Highlighting the importance of being an informed citizen of tomorrow’s world, The NEST School’s brilliant CAS (Creativity, Activity, Science) programme brings learners one step closer to hands-on experiences. By actively engaging in volunteerism and helping create lasting social impact, learners work with local communities to address their needs and provide support through various programs and initiatives.
